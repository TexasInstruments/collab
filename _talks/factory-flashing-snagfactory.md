## About this talk

This talk examines the challenges of flashing embedded devices at scale on the factory floor and how existing tools and workflows can be streamlined. It covers the pain points of traditional factory flashing processes and how a more automated, "snag it, flash it, ship it" approach can reduce friction. The speakers, from Bootlin and Texas Instruments, bring both open-source tooling and silicon vendor perspectives to the discussion. Attendees can expect insight into practical tradeoffs involved in building a reliable, repeatable production flashing pipeline.
