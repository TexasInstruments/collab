## About this talk

This talk discusses how Texas Instruments extends upstream Linux kernel quality assurance for its SoCs beyond the coverage already provided by kernelci.org. It explores gaps in existing continuous integration and testing infrastructure and the additional validation TI performs internally. The speakers, both from TI, share their perspective on building confidence in upstream kernel releases for embedded and industrial use cases. The talk is of interest to engineers working on kernel testing, CI pipelines, and long-term maintenance of SoC support in mainline Linux.
